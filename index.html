About Us

Our Story

Founded in 2020, Yume Bookstore (夢 means "dream" in Japanese) started as a small passion project to bring the best manga and anime literature to our community. What began as a tiny shop has grown into a beloved destination for manga enthusiasts.

Our Mission

To provide manga lovers with an extensive collection of high-quality manga, create a welcoming community space, and inspire readers through the power of Japanese storytelling.

Our Vision

To become the leading manga bookstore that connects readers with their favorite stories and introduces new fans to the wonderful world of manga.


Location: Manggahan Street, Encarnacion, Calatagan, Batangas, Philippines
